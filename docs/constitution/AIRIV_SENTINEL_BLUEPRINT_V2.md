# Constitutional Statement

AIRIV Sentinel is the canonical autonomous engineering platform of the AIRIV ecosystem.

AIRIV Sentinel is **not** an AI model.

AIRIV Sentinel is **not** a chatbot.

AIRIV Sentinel is **not** a coding assistant.

AIRIV Sentinel is a **Linux-first Autonomous Multi-AI Engineering Desktop** capable of planning, executing, verifying, and completing engineering missions by orchestrating multiple AI reasoning providers while preserving its own engineering identity.

This document is the highest governing document of the AIRIV Sentinel project.

Every implementation shall conform to this Blueprint.

Implementations may evolve.

Product identity shall not.

---

# Mission

Build the world's most accessible autonomous engineering desktop.

Accessibility means:

- Linux-first
- AI-provider independent
- No mandatory API subscriptions
- Supports free and paid AI resources
- Affordable by design
- Mission driven
- Engineering focused

# Core Principles

## Linux First

Linux is the canonical execution platform.

Windows is outside the scope of Blueprint v2.

macOS is outside the scope of Blueprint v2.

---

## Mission First

Everything begins with a Mission.

Tasks exist only to complete Missions.

---

## AI Independence

Sentinel must never depend on one AI provider.

Supported providers may include:

- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- DeepSeek
- Ollama
- Hugging Face
- OpenRouter
- Future providers

Providers are interchangeable reasoning resources.

---

## Engineering Ownership

Sentinel owns engineering.

AI assists engineering.

Sentinel never delegates ownership of engineering decisions to any AI provider.

---

## Reality First

Observed reality overrides assumptions.

Evidence overrides confidence.

Verification overrides narrative.

Unknown remains Unknown until verified.

---

## Verification First

No mission is complete until independently verified.

---

## No Vendor Lock-in

Sentinel must remain portable.

Sentinel identity shall never depend on:

- proprietary APIs
- proprietary models
- proprietary subscriptions
- proprietary workflows
